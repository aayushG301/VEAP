# Suspected heart attack
id: heart_attack
keywords: chest pain, heart attack, chest pressure, left arm pain

1. Call your local emergency number immediately.
2. Keep the person seated or semi-reclined, calm and still.
3. Loosen tight clothing. Do not let them walk or drive.
4. Only give aspirin if the emergency dispatcher advises it AND the person is not allergic and not on blood thinners.
5. Be ready to start CPR if they become unresponsive.
