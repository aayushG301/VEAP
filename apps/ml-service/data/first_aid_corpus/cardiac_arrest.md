# Unresponsive and not breathing normally (suspected cardiac arrest)
id: cardiac_arrest
keywords: unconscious, unresponsive, not breathing, cardiac arrest, no pulse, collapsed

1. Call your local emergency number now (or ask someone to) and ask for an AED.
2. Start chest compressions: heel of hand on the centre of the chest, push hard and fast (about 100-120 per minute, 5-6 cm deep for adults), allow full recoil.
3. Use an AED as soon as it arrives and follow its voice prompts.
4. Do not stop until professionals take over or the person starts breathing normally.
