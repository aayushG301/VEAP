# Seizure
id: seizure
keywords: seizure, convulsion, fit, epilepsy

1. Note the time. Move hard or sharp objects away.
2. Cushion the head. Do not restrain and do not put anything in the mouth.
3. When the shaking stops, place the person on their side (recovery position).
4. Call emergency services if it lasts over 5 minutes, repeats, it is a first seizure, there is injury, or breathing does not return to normal.
