# Low blood sugar (diabetic emergency)
id: hypoglycemia
keywords: diabetic, low blood sugar, hypoglycemia, insulin, sugar low

1. If conscious and able to swallow: give 15-20 g fast sugar (glucose tablets or juice).
2. Recheck in 15 minutes and repeat if still unwell.
3. If drowsy, unconscious or unable to swallow: give nothing by mouth, place on their side and call emergency services.
