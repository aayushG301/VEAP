# Choking adult (cannot cough, speak or breathe)
id: choking
keywords: choking, choke, cannot breathe food, something stuck in throat

1. Ask: are you choking? If they can cough or speak, encourage coughing.
2. If they cannot: give up to 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades.
3. Then up to 5 abdominal thrusts (stand behind, fist above navel, pull inwards and upwards).
4. Alternate 5 back blows and 5 thrusts. Call emergency services if not cleared.
5. If they become unresponsive, start CPR.
