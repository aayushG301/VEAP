# Severe bleeding
id: bleeding
keywords: bleeding, blood loss, cut, stab, wound, hemorrhage

1. Call your local emergency number for heavy bleeding.
2. Apply firm, direct pressure with a clean cloth. Do not remove soaked cloth; add more on top.
3. Keep the person lying down and warm. Raise the limb if no fracture is suspected.
4. If a limb is bleeding uncontrollably and you are trained, apply a tourniquet above the wound.
